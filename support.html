Getting started

  • Open your diary: tap the cover.
  • Make a page: tap Make a Page (the pencil-and-square button in the top right corner), choose up to three photos, add a few words, and tap Make It Beautiful.
  • Turn pages: drag from the edge of the page, or swipe sideways on empty paper.
  • Move things: tap a photo, note, or sticker, then drag it. Use two fingers to resize or tilt it.
  • Write, draw, and add stickers: use the buttons at the bottom of the page.
  • Photo booth: on an iPhone with the Dynamic Island, pull down from the island while a page is open.
  • More diaries: tap Diaries in the top left corner to see your shelf and start a new one.

Common questions

Where are my pages saved?
On your iPhone, automatically. There's no account and no cloud sync. Your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup can include your diary. If you delete the app, your diaries are deleted too, so save a PDF first if you want to keep them.

How do I keep or print my whole diary?
Open Settings (the gear in the top left corner) and tap Save Diary as PDF, or open All Pages (the list button in the top left corner) and tap the share button. You can save the PDF to Files, print it, or send it. Tapebook doesn't keep a copy.

Can I lock my diary?
Yes. In Settings, under Privacy, turn on Lock with Face ID (or Touch ID or your passcode). Your pages also stay hidden in the App Switcher.

I deleted something by mistake.
Tap Undo in the note that appears right after you delete a photo, note, or sticker. It stays for a few seconds. To undo a move or an edit, tap the Undo arrow at the top of the page; touch and hold it to redo.

Make a Page, Write About It, or Sketch doesn't write or draw anything.
These features need Apple Intelligence. Make sure it's turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri, that your iPhone supports it, and that your iPhone's language is English. Apple limits how many sketches and cloud requests you can make each day; if you reach the limit, try again tomorrow. Without Apple Intelligence, Make a Page still builds a page from your photos and words.

Can I change how the notes sound?
Yes. Open Settings and tap Note Style. You can also change the pen, the paper, and your cover there.

I don't like how a made page looks.
Open the More menu (•••) and tap Another Look. Your words stay the same; the paper, pens, and layout change.

Can Tapebook remind me to write?
Yes. In Settings, under Reminder, turn on Daily Reminder and choose a time. It skips days you've already made a page, and it never shows what's in your diary.

How do I share a page?
Open the More menu (•••) and tap Share Page. Choose Save Image to put it in Photos.
