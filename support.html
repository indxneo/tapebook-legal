Getting started

  • Open your diary: tap the cover.
  • Make a page: tap New Page in the top right corner, pick up to 3 photos, add a few words and tap Make It Beautiful.
  • Turn pages: drag from the edge of the page, or swipe sideways on empty paper.
  • Move things: tap a photo, note or sticker, then drag it. Use two fingers to resize or turn it.
  • Write or draw: use the buttons at the bottom of the page.
  • More diaries: tap Diaries in the top left corner to see your shelf and start a new one.

Common questions

Where are my pages saved?
On your iPhone, automatically. There's no account and no cloud sync. Your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup can include your diary. If you delete the app, your diaries are deleted too.

I deleted something by mistake.
Tap Undo in the note that appears right after you delete a photo, note or sticker. It stays for a few seconds.

Make a Page, Write About It or Sketch doesn't write or draw anything.
These features need Apple Intelligence. Make sure it's turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri and that your iPhone supports it. Apple limits how many sketches and cloud requests you can make each day; if you reach the limit, try again tomorrow. Without Apple Intelligence, Make a Page still builds a page from your photos and words.

Can I change how the notes sound?
Yes. Open Settings (the gear in the top left corner) and choose a Note Style. You can also change the pen, the paper and your cover there.

I don't like how a made page looks.
Open the More menu and tap Another Look. Your words stay the same; the paper, pens and layout change.

How do I share a page?
Open the More menu and tap Share Page.
