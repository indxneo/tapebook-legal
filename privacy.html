Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

The short version: Your diary stays on your iPhone. Tapebook has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't run any servers. We never see your photos, notes or drawings. When you use the app's Apple Intelligence features, your photos and words go to Apple's models to do that one job, as described below.

What we collect

Nothing. Tapebook doesn't send any information to us or to anyone else for advertising, analytics or tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email address, contacts or location, and it doesn't create an advertising identifier.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you put in your diary is saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • Photos you add. When you pick photos, the app only gets the photos you choose, not your whole library. Photos you take with the camera inside Tapebook are kept only in your diary.
  • What you make: notes, drawings, sketches, stickers, page dates, diary names and cover pictures.
  • Your answers to the short questions the app asks about a photo, and notes you've edited, so new notes can sound like you.
  • Your choices: pen, paper, note style, cover color, sounds and other settings.

To find faces and what a photo shows (so words aren't placed over people), the app uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs on your iPhone.

Apple Intelligence

Some features use Apple Intelligence, built into iOS: Make a Page, Write About It, Rewrite and Sketch. They only run when you tap them. For these features, the photos and words involved (for example, the words you typed and your answers) are processed by Apple's models, either on your iPhone or with Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Apple says Private Cloud Compute uses your data only to complete your request and doesn't store it. Sketch uses Apple's Image Playground. These features are provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive any of this data.

If Apple Intelligence is off or isn't available on your iPhone, Tapebook still makes pages without it, on your iPhone.

Sharing

A page leaves the app only when you choose to share it with the Share button. Where it goes then is up to you and the app you share it with.

Backups and deleting

Tapebook doesn't sync your diary to any cloud. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, your diary may be included in that backup, under Apple's privacy policy. You can delete pages and diaries in the app. Deleting the app deletes your diaries from your iPhone.

Third parties

Tapebook contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. When you download the app, Apple handles the download and may collect information as described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Tapebook collects no information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version of Tapebook changes any of this, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
